What the problem looked like

Orders grew quickly, but trust began to decay. Supply gaps, delayed fulfillment, and low inventory confidence were causing preventable cancellations and lower repeat business.

  • ✓ Availability was not visible before checkout.
  • ✓ Customers could not compare store confidence or ETA quality.
  • ✓ Store teams lacked a clear exception queue for missing items.

How NOVA fixes it

By surfacing live inventory confidence, reliability rankings, and operational alerts, the system helps both shoppers and stores make better decisions before the order is confirmed.

  • ✓ Rank stores using reliability, distance, ETA, and inventory confidence.
  • ✓ Surface inventory exceptions before they turn into failed deliveries.
  • ✓ Give customers a transparent view of order confidence before payment.
